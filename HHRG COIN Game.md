# Police attacks

When police attack in a space, they roll a dice, and add that value to the number of police in the space. The government player must remove that many pieces, if possible. Active rats are removed first, then gang members, then civilians, then underground rats. An underground rat counts as 2 pieces for the sake of removal, and can't be halfway removed.

If any civilians are removed by a police attack, add terror to the space (if none), and set the space to resist.

# Rat attacks

When rats attack police, they must remove at least one arms cache in the target space or an adjacent space, and reveal an underground rat in the target space.

The rats roll a die and add the number of arms caches removed, to determine their attack power. Removing a police requires 2 power. If all police are removed and power remains, remove one of each piece listed next in rotating order: politician, civilian, gang member.

Rat attacks always place terror. If a civilian is removed by a rat attack, set the space to support.

# Politicians

Whenever a politician is removed by a rat attack, the rats instantly gain points, and an emergency zone is placed in the space.

At a propaganda round, any surviving politicians give the government points.

# Terror

Terror causes shootouts to occur.

# Emergency zones

The government player may spend resources to place probes in emergency zones. Probes do not contribute to attacks, but help reveal underground rats in sweeps.

# Moss growers and arms caches

The rats player can place moss growers in spaces with resist with a special activity or something. Moss growers produce resources necessary to create arms caches.

If police attack in a space with a moss grower, they will never remove civilians. If police remove all rats and gang members in a space with a moss grower, the moss grower is removed, and the space is set to support.

# Shootouts

Shootouts can be triggered by certain actions and propaganda rounds. A shootout involves rats, gang members, and civilians (not police). Shootouts affect both underground and active rats (underground rats are not protected).

Shootouts occur in any spaces with terror. If the space is at support (supports government), gang members and civilians both target rats.

If the space is at neutral, civilians will not attack, but gang members will still attack rats.

If a space is at resist (sides with rats), gang members and civilians don't attack.
