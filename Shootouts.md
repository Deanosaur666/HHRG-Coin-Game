Shootouts can occur in spaces with gangsters or terror.

Shootout "power" is gangsters + terror markers.

In propaganda, check for a shootout in every space with gangsters or terror.

When checking for a shootout, roll a dice; if the result equal to or under power, the shootout will occur.

Remove pieces equal to power, in alternating order:
- Neutral cube (first civilians, then gangsters, then militia)
- Active rat (or reveal underground rat if none active)

The last neutral cube in a space will never be removed by a shootout.

Any active politicians in the space are flipped underground.
