## Double Sided Emergency Zones

"Passive" and "Active" emergency zones.
Emergency zones start by being placed passively.
All bombings will place an emergency zone, or, if one exists, flip it to active.
A passive emergency zone helps with sweeps and can reveal rats when moving in.
An active emergency zone adds the capability of restricting movement out, and prevents recruiting in that space.

Police can use mobilize to flip an emergency zone from passive to active.
