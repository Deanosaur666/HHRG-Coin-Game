## Double Sided Emergency Zones

Sweeping ALWAYS lets you place an emergency zone.

"Passive" and "Active" emergency zones.
Emergency zones start by being placed passively.
All bombings will place an emergency zone, or, if one exists, flip it to active.
A passive emergency zone helps with sweeps, can reveal rats when moving in, and prevents recruiting in that space.
An active emergency zone adds the capability of restricting movement out. In propagada round, every rat piece in an active emergency zone will be removed. Sweeping into an active gives the opportunity to assault, or reveal every rat cell.

Police can use mobilize to flip an emergency zone from passive to active.

Sabotage in already opposing spaces can degrade emergency zones. (Active->Passive->Remove)
