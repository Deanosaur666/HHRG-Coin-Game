
# Econ

Instead of an "event" space, will have an "econ" action for the middle space.

Passing lets the player take a "limited econ action."

For police, "Econ" lets them place multiple armored cars. Passing places one.


For rats, "Econ" lets them increase the moss track and place arms caches. Passing either increases moss or places one arms cache.


# Ops

## Police

Train.
In non-sabotaged spaces, either:
- Place 2 police officers from supply.
- Place a politician and convert a gangster to a civilian.
- In a space with a politician, convert a number of gangsters to civilians based on support.

Sweep
Move police along a path adjacent spaces, stopping at sabotage. Then, at final destination spaces:
- If sabotaged, reveal 1 rat for every 2 police,
- Otherwise, reveal 1 rat per police.

Assault
In each space, determine attack power by rolling a dice and adding the number of police in the space.
- First remove act rats, then arms caches/moss grower, then **alternate** gangsters and civilians, then underground rats.
- Underground rats require 1 hit to reveal, a second to kill.
- If an arms cache is removed, gain 1 political will.
- If civilians removed, shift the support track down a space, and add a terror.

## Rats

Rally.
In spaces without politicians:
- Place 1 rat
- Then, may convert 1 gangster to a rat.
- At arms cache, place an extra rat. (Up to 3 rats total)

Travel
Move Rat cells from origin spaces to adjacent spaces. Then:
* If destination is an LOC, or is an emergency zone, and moving cells plus cubes there exceed 3, flip moving cells to active.
* Otherwise, flip moving cells underground.

Attack
At spaces with underground cells, reveal a cell, then either:
- Remove (kill) a politician if there are no police in the space (see **Assassination**).
- Bomb the space. Remove one or more arms caches in the space or adjacent to it. Determine attack power by rolling a die and adding 2 for each arms cache removed.
	* First, remove police cubes (each cube costs 2 power, removed to out of play).
	* Then, once no police, if there is an armored car there or in an adjacent LOC, you may remove it for 2 power. If an armored car is removed, may place an arms cache in an adjacent space.
	* Then, if power remains, remove (kill) the following pieces in alternating order (at a cost of 1 power each): politician, civilian cube, gang cube. (See **Assassination**). If any civilian cubes were removed, shift the support track to the right.
Then, if the space is an LOC, add a sabotage, otherwise add a terror.

Sabotage
At spaces with underground cells, reveal a cell, then place a sabotage.
