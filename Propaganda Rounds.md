# Government phases
Perform the "armored car movement" and "artillery" phases. Do not perform the drug trade phase.

# Shootouts

In every space with gangsters or terror, roll for a shootout ([[Shootouts]]).

# Bombing

Rats may perform one free bombing (attack option B).

# Score population

Score the difference in population (both civilians in gangsters) between districts at support and oppose.

Increase political will by 1 for each space at support with an active politician.

# Score Collateral

Compare the number of civilians  in the collateral box for each side. Halve that value, rounding up. Add to political will if rats have more, subtract from political will if the government have more.

Then, move all collateral to available.
# Score sabotage

Decrease political will by the economic value of each sabotaged strategic center (factories, police stations, and artillery) and each sabotaged LOC.

# Last round game end

If this is the last propaganda round, the game ends here.

# Politicians

In each space with an active politician, for each active politician, may replace one population cube with a civilian or a militia. Then, the space shifts one step towards support.

Then, flip all politicians active.

# Propagandize
In each space with at least one rat cell, no police, politicans, or emergency zones, shift one step towards resist.

# Repopulate
(Start with the NW district, and go left-to right top to bottom, in the case that population is not available.)
In every district, repopulate if under 1 population, or under two population for central districts (NC and CC).  Add a gangster if there is a terror, or no civilians available. Otherwise, add a civilian.

# Redeploy
Police must move from oppose spaces to police stations.

Politicians in oppose spaces are returned to available.

Rats in emergency zones are returned to available.

Rats must move from strategic centers and LOCs to districts without support, or foreign country spaces.

# Reset
Flip all guerrillas underground.
Remove all sabotage, terror and emergency zones from the map.
Return half (rounded down) of politicians from out of play to available.
If the artillery marker is out of play, return it to available.
