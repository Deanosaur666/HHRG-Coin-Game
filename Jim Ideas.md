Police have armored supply trucks that come down LOCs.

Armored supply trucks will add to available police.

When police are destroyed, they go out of play.

The gang player can use arms caches to bomb armored trucks. This can help keep the police player's supply of police low. (This represents destroying supplies needed to arm more police.)

As the opposite of some COIN games, it could be the government player that suffers more with piece supplies, reflecting how expensive their forces are.
